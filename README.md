# Metal America

**Physical AI for construction.**

Metal America is a physical AI lab for construction. We build thousands of real construction projects across the United States with a national network of tradesmen, and we capture that work as first-person video tied to what each job cost, how long it took and whether it passed inspection. We use this data to publish research on AI and robotics for construction, license construction training data to robotics labs, and bring robots onto real job sites.

## Research

Our [physical AI research](https://metalamerica.ai/research) is tested on real jobs with known outcomes, not demos.

| Study | What we found |
|---|---|
| The seven pillars of physical AI | Self-driving built seven pillars in order: fleet, real-world operation, ground-truth data engine, planning, sensors at scale, model testing and deployment. Construction robotics needs the same seven, and four are already in place. |
| Construction AI eval: 387 real questions | Six frontier and open LLMs averaged about 19% correct on their own and about 90% with retrieved company data. The data mattered more than the model. |
| Install sequence graph | A 40-step task graph for a concrete slab and a steel building (21 + 19 steps), used as the annotation schema for crew video. |
| VLMs for construction progress | Five frontier vision-language models named the build stage in real job photos 88 to 100% of the time. |
| AI data annotation | A five-model majority vote labels job-site photos with 98% accuracy. |
| Site slope from phone photos | Phone-photo slope estimates compared with USGS 3DEP elevation data on 106 slab sites. |
